Highlights

Rank an epic’s child issues in one place.

Jira lets you drag sub-tasks into order, but not an epic’s child issues in company-managed spaces. Issue Order fills that gap.

Reorder

Drag an epic’s child issues into order

Open one epic, or a whole space as one numbered outline. Tick several issues to drag them together, even in company-managed spaces, where Jira can’t.

Move to epic

Move child issues to another epic

Drop ticked issues onto another epic, or pick one with Move to…, even in another company-managed space. Or sort them by priority, due date or story points.

Review

Review, then save in one go

Each change shows its new number, the old one struck through, and where it came from. One save writes them all to Jira.

1,000 issues a listEach list shows its first 1,000 issues in Rank order. Hide done keeps finished work out of the way.
Your permissionsIt acts as you, so people only reorder issues they could already rank on a board.
No databaseNothing is kept on a server. Unsaved changes wait in your browser until you save.
Runs on AtlassianBuilt on Forge — no external servers, no data leaving Jira.
