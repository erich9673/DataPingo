Highlights

Every page in its place, in one step.

Confluence adds new pages to the end of the list and moves them one drag at a time. Page Order does the whole list at once.

Sort

Sort child pages in one step

A to Z, Z to A, newest, oldest or recently updated — for the pages you pick, or every level below. Numbers sort as numbers, so 2 comes before 10.

Drag and Drop

Drag many pages into place at once

Drag across pages to select a group, then drop it on a new spot or onto another page. Nothing changes in Confluence until you save.

Move To…

Move pages to another parent or space

Pick any space you can reach and the page to put them under. Child pages go with their parent, and a clashing title can be renamed on the way.
