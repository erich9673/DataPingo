ISSUE ORDER FOR JIRA

Limits

Every ceiling in one place, Jira’s own rules included, and what happens at each.

Limits

A few numbers are Issue Order for Jira’s own. The rest are Jira’s rules: the app works within them, and checks them before a change is staged, so a refusal doesn’t first appear when you save.

1,000 issues per list

Each list (a space’s epics, an epic’s child issues, an issue’s sub-tasks, Without an epic) shows its first 1,000 issues in Rank order. A list with more:

  • Says so. It ends with Only the first 1,000 issues by Rank are shown here., and a banner above the outline reads Some lists have more than 1,000 issues. The first 1,000 are shown, and Sort is off for those lists.
  • Can still be reordered among the issues shown, by dragging or from a row’s ••• menu.
  • Can’t be sorted. The Sort bar says This list has more than 1,000 issues, so Sort is off for it. When a sort covers several lists, the others are sorted and the status says Lists with more than 1,000 issues were left as they are.
  • Can’t take issues at its end, because its end isn’t on screen: This list has more than 1,000 issues and only the first 1,000 are shown, so its end isn’t here. Drop them next to one you can see instead. At the top still works.
  • Can’t be put in lanes: Lists with more than 1,000 issues can’t be put in lanes here.

Hide done helps. With Hide done on, a list past 1,000 is read again without its done issues, so years of finished work don’t crowd out what’s open. Its last row then reads Only the first 1,000 open issues by Rank are shown here. Switching Hide done reloads those lists, but not while you have unsaved changes: Lists with more than 1,000 issues will switch once your changes are saved or discarded.

Under epics in other spaces counts only issues whose epic is in another space toward its 1,000. In a space with more than 2,000 epics, the app finds them among the space’s first 20,000 child issues, in Rank order.

Move to… shows up to 50 results

A search in Move to… lists up to 50 matches, most recently updated first. A whole key such as SHOP-1 finds that issue; other words match the start of words in a summary. With the search empty and Other spaces off, a child issue’s choices are its space’s epics in the order the outline shows them, then Without an epic. In the pop-up, where the space’s epics aren’t loaded, they come from Jira instead: up to 50, most recently updated first.

With Hide done on, done epics and other done parents aren’t offered, and when that leaves nothing to offer the note says why, for example No open epics here. Done ones are hidden while Hide done is on.

One site and one space at a time

Issue Order works on the Jira site it’s installed on. The Apps page shows one space at a time, and switching to another with unsaved changes asks first. The pop-up shows the issue it was opened on, and zooms out to that issue’s space. Unsaved changes belong to the site, person and starting point they were made on, so drafts in two spaces never mix. Cross-space links still show: another space’s issue under one of this space’s epics is listed with that epic’s child issues, and this space’s issues under another space’s epic are in Under epics in other spaces.

Batch sizes that don’t limit you

These numbers shape how the app talks to Jira. None of them caps what you see or change:

  • Searches ask for issue ids only, up to 5,000 a page, Jira’s most. One search covers up to 50 parents; if their children come to more than 5,000, each parent is read on its own.
  • Issues are read 100 to a call, Jira’s most, up to 8 calls at once.
  • A rank call moves up to 50 issues, Jira’s most, so a big sort is several calls.
  • New parents and new priorities are written four at a time.

Jira’s own rules

These come from Jira and apply to every way of changing an issue, not just this app, so the app can’t lift them. It checks them as you drag or choose in Move to…: a choice Jira would refuse is greyed out with the reason, and a drop is refused with it.

Across spaces

Company-managed spaces share epics. A child issue can go under an epic in another company-managed space, with Other spaces ticked in Move to…. In its own space it then shows under Under epics in other spaces, where it can be reordered, or moved out to one of the space’s own epics. Its current epic, if offered again, shows Already here.

Team-managed spaces keep parents and children inside the space, in both directions. Move to… doesn’t offer Other spaces for their issues, and a move into or out of one is refused: Team-managed spaces keep parents and children in the same space.

Sub-tasks never change space, in either kind.
