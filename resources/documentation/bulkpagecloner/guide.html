On this page

Bulk Page Cloner turns one page into up to 25 named copies in a four-step flow — Source, Copies, Location, Done — with a progress bar at the top. From the Copies step onward a live clone summary shows your source, copy count and destination, so the whole job is visible before any of it happens.
